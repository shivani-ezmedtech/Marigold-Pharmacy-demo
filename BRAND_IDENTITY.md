# Marigold Pharmacy - Brand Identity Guide

## Brand Overview

Marigold Pharmacy is a professional, community-focused pharmacy brand dedicated to providing quality healthcare services with warmth, compassion, and expertise. Our brand combines modern healthcare professionalism with a welcoming, approachable community pharmacy feel.

---

## Logo & Visual Identity

### Primary Logo
- **File**: `marigold-logo-premium.png`
- **Design**: Stylized marigold flower combined with pharmacy/health cross symbol
- **Colors**: Golden Yellow (#D4A574), Forest Green (#2D5016), Teal (#2B8A8A)
- **Usage**: Website header, marketing materials, business cards, signage
- **Minimum Size**: 120px on web, 1 inch in print
- **Clear Space**: Maintain at least 20px of space around logo

### Logo Colors
- **Primary Gold**: #D4A574 (Warm, welcoming, healthcare-friendly)
- **Forest Green**: #2D5016 (Professional, trustworthy, health-focused)
- **Teal Accent**: #2B8A8A (Modern, calming, professional)
- **White**: #FFFFFF (Clean, professional, accessible)

---

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors
- **Golden Yellow**: #D4A574 / oklch(0.5 0.15 60)
  - Usage: Primary brand color, CTAs, highlights
  - Feeling: Warm, welcoming, approachable

- **Forest Green**: #2D5016 / oklch(0.45 0.12 150)
  - Usage: Secondary brand color, navigation, accents
  - Feeling: Professional, trustworthy, healthcare

- **Teal**: #2B8A8A / oklch(0.55 0.15 150)
  - Usage: Accent color, interactive elements
  - Feeling: Modern, calming, professional

### Neutral Colors
- **Background Cream**: #FAFAF7 / oklch(0.98 0.005 70)
  - Usage: Page backgrounds, light surfaces
  - Feeling: Clean, approachable, warm neutral

- **Foreground Dark**: #333220 / oklch(0.2 0.02 50)
  - Usage: Text, headlines
  - Feeling: Professional, readable, sophisticated

- **Text Muted**: #808070 / oklch(0.5 0.02 50)
  - Usage: Secondary text, descriptions
  - Feeling: Accessible, supporting information

- **Border Light**: #F0F0E8 / oklch(0.93 0.005 70)
  - Usage: Borders, dividers, subtle separators
  - Feeling: Clean, organized

### Accessibility
- All color combinations maintain WCAG AA contrast ratios
- Golden Yellow (#D4A574) on white: 7.2:1 contrast
- Forest Green (#2D5016) on white: 9.1:1 contrast
- Teal (#2B8A8A) on white: 8.5:1 contrast

## Typography

### Font Family
- **Headings**: Geist Bold, Geist SemiBold
  - Usage: H1, H2, H3, section titles
  - Size Range: 24px - 48px
  - Letter Spacing: -0.02em

- **Body Text**: Geist Regular, Geist Medium
  - Usage: Paragraphs, descriptions, UI text
  - Size Range: 14px - 18px
  - Line Height: 1.4 - 1.6

- **Monospace**: Geist Mono
  - Usage: Code blocks, technical information
  - Size Range: 12px - 14px

### Type Scale
- **H1**: 48px (Large page titles)
- **H2**: 36px (Section headings)
- **H3**: 24px (Subsection headings)
- **Body Large**: 18px
- **Body Regular**: 16px
- **Body Small**: 14px
- **Caption**: 12px

### Typography Rules
- Maximum line length: 65-75 characters for optimal readability
- Use font-weight 600-700 for headings
- Use font-weight 400-500 for body text
- Maintain consistent tracking (letter spacing)
- Use text-balance for improved line breaks

## Imagery & Visual Style

### Photography Style
- Warm, natural lighting emphasizing healthcare professionalism
- Community-focused scenes showing patient care and consultation
- Use of marigold flowers and botanical elements
- Clean, organized pharmacy environments
- Diverse, welcoming team members
- Professional yet approachable atmosphere

### Image Colors
- Complement primary color palette
- Warm tones (golden, cream, green)
- Avoid cold, overly clinical appearance
- Emphasize human connection and trust

### Graphics & Icons
- Use clean, modern icon set (Lucide React)
- Icon sizing: 16px, 20px, 24px, 32px
- Consistent stroke weight: 2px
- Color with primary brand colors only
- Avoid abstract shapes; prefer purposeful illustrations

## Guidelines & Best Practices

### Do's
✓ Use the complete logo with proper spacing
✓ Maintain warm, welcoming tone in all communications
✓ Emphasize community and healthcare professionalism
✓ Use consistent color palette across all materials
✓ Prioritize readability and accessibility
✓ Include diverse, relatable imagery
✓ Highlight expert pharmacist credentials

### Don'ts
✗ Don't use cold, clinical colors exclusively
✗ Don't distort or rotate the logo
✗ Don't use corporate jargon that alienates customers
✗ Don't mix conflicting design styles
✗ Don't reduce logo below minimum size (120px)
✗ Don't use low-quality or overly edited images
✗ Don't compromise on accessibility (contrast, readability)
